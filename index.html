QUALITY DECISIONS / PHYSICAL WORK / HUMAN-GOVERNED

Make the work
reviewable.

Earthward Foundry is developing a narrow evidence-led quality and traceability pilot for one high-mix, low-volume manufacturing part family. The aim is not to automate acceptance. It is to make the evidence, hold, correction, and accountable human decision easier to reconstruct.

Inspect the proposed pilot ↓Follow the work loop →

THE PUBLIC QUESTION “Could another person reconstruct why this part was held, reworked, or accepted—without asking the person who happened to be there?”

01 / THE WORK LOOP

From requirement
to a useful record.

Earthward’s first public focus is deliberately narrow: one discrete-manufacturing part family and the quality evidence around its release-to-acceptance loop.

  1. 01

    Release the part record

    Begin with the relevant part and revision context, rather than creating a record after a problem emerges.

  2. 02

    Attach inspection evidence

    Keep an observed inspection result tied to the part record so it cannot quietly migrate between revisions or outcomes.

  3. 03

    Expose the hold

    When a nonconformance or missing fact appears, make the condition visible instead of presenting an incomplete record as complete.

  4. 04

    Record the correction

    Capture the reason and accountable authority for a rework or disposition event in an append-only sequence.

  5. 05

    Keep acceptance human

    The final irreversible acceptance decision belongs to the responsible person, with evidence available for a later review.

PROPOSED FIRST OFFER / EVIDENCE-LEDGER PILOT

One facility.
One part family.
One evidence loop.

The proposed initial pilot serves a Quality Engineer or records specialist, with adjacent manufacturing and inspection roles. It is a configured, human-governed implementation—not self-service software and not a promise of broad agent coverage.

02 / WHAT THIS CHANGES

A record is not
a rubber stamp.

Public language should distinguish evidence management from the standards authority, specialist judgment, or calibration chain needed to support a particular measurement claim.

VISIBLE

Gap, hold, or uncertainty

The design preserves the difference between a missing step and a passing result.

ATTRIBUTABLE

Correction and decision

An append-only event should make the change and responsible role inspectable later.

PORTABLE

Record and export

The proposed pilot starts with a structured record, not a platform claim or locked-in data story.

HUMAN-HELD

Acceptance and exception

Tools can organize evidence; accountable people retain consequential decisions.
