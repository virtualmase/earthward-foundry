# Earthward Foundry Public Property: Design Direction

## Three directions considered

| Theme | Brief intent | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| **Heat and Ledger** | An editorial foundry field book in oxidized iron, paper, and calibration blue. It makes the record feel as material as the work. | 0.07 |
| **Measurement Bench** | A quiet instrument-panel composition that privileges tolerances, evidence, and inspection. It risks looking like an internal dashboard. | 0.04 |
| **Workshop Ledger** | A physical-work journal that moves from requirement to record through measured stages. It places a human decision gate at the center rather than depicting automation as the hero. | 0.08 |

## Chosen direction: Workshop Ledger

**Design movement:** Material-informed editorial systems design, influenced by workshop marks, inspection sheets, and restrained field notes rather than industrial software marketing.

**Core principles:** Let physical work and its evidence carry the visual weight; make the public/private boundary visible; use process lines to connect stages without suggesting a fully automated factory; reserve emphasis for the accountable human gate.

**Color philosophy:** Mineral paper creates an inspection surface; furnace rust names the decision or hold boundary; graphite carries working information; calibration blue marks linked evidence and public navigation. Color never represents pass/fail, safety, or certification.

**Layout paradigm:** A staggered workbench of evidence strips, process cards, and a central decision seam. The structure begins with a physical-work narrative, then resolves into a public source/correction footer—not a centered SaaS funnel.

**Signature elements:** An original `requirement → record` chain, a rust human-decision pin, and ruled inspection-sheet metadata throughout the page.

**Interaction philosophy:** No conversion gimmicks or simulated product controls. Links are direct and reader-labelled. The page remains fully useful without a form, account, or tracking.

**Animation:** No required animation. Hover and focus only reveal link intent through a short color/line transition; reduced motion retains the same information.

**Typography system:** `Georgia`/`Iowan Old Style` provides the decision voice; `Arial`/system sans provides working notes; `ui-monospace` provides inspection labels and record codes. All fonts are system fallbacks; no remote font dependency.

**Brand essence:** Evidence-led quality records for physical work that keeps human acceptance visible. **Personality:** grounded, exacting, durable.

**Brand voice:** Headlines are declarative and material; CTAs identify the record a reader will inspect. Examples: “Make the work reviewable.” and “Inspect the proposed pilot boundary.”

**Wordmark and mark:** A simple rectangular workpiece split by a rust record seam, with a small calibration-blue witness point. The wordmark uses letter-spaced utility type, not a default logo font.

**Signature brand color:** `#B54A2B` — **Foundry Rust**, reserved for a hold, decision, or record seam.
