# Director-of-Quality Outreach: $15,000 Evidence-Ledger Pilot

## Positioning guardrails

The message should sell a **scoped evidence workflow**, not an AI promise. Do not claim existing customer results, audit outcomes, certification, regulatory compliance, or time savings that Earthward has not yet measured. Personalize only with a public, relevant fact about the prospect’s part complexity, quality posture, or manufacturing process. Do not imply that the recipient has an evidence problem unless they confirm it.

## Primary email

**Subject:** A reviewable acceptance record for one critical part family

Hi **[First Name]**,

When a customer, auditor, or internal review asks why a part was released, the answer is often spread across inspection reports, NCRs, rework notes, shared drives, and approval threads.

I’m building **Earthward Foundry**, a human-governed Evidence Ledger for one high-consequence part family. It does not replace a QMS or give software authority to release a part. It creates a complete, append-only record from inspection through deviation, rework, and named human acceptance—then produces a reviewable evidence package.

We are opening one **$15,000 design-partner pilot** for one facility and one part family. It includes workflow configuration, initial data import, operator rollout, and a usable export for quality review.

If complete acceptance records are a recurring operational concern at **[Company]**, would a 20-minute conversation next week be useful to determine whether the pilot fits your process?

Best,

**[Sender Name]**  
**Earthward Foundry**  
**[Phone] | [Calendar Link]**

## Personalization line options

Insert exactly one fact-based sentence after the greeting. Remove it if no credible public fact is available.

| Situation | Personalization line |
|---|---|
| Complex or precision parts | “I noticed that [Company] works on [specific public product/process]; preserving the decision trail around inspection and release seems especially relevant in that environment.” |
| Public quality credential | “I saw that [Company] publicly references [credential or quality system]; I am reaching out because the pilot is intended to make the supporting production record easier to retrieve and review.” |
| Supplier or regulated industry | “Your work in [publicly stated sector] appears to make clear evidence around inspection, deviations, and release especially important.” |

## Call objective

The outreach is successful if the recipient agrees to a discovery conversation, not if they buy on the first exchange. In the call, validate the current evidence path for one part family, the responsible roles, the record-retrieval pain, the systems already in use, and the definition of a useful exported package. Do not position the pilot as a replacement for the existing QMS, MES, or quality authority.
